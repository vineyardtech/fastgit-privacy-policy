FastGit ("we", "our", "us", or "the app"), developed and operated by Vineyard Tech, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, usage, and safeguarding of information when you download, install, and use the FastGit mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Handle It

  • GitHub Authentication & Account Data: When you sign in to FastGit using GitHub OAuth or Device Authorization Flow, authentication is handled directly through GitHub’s official servers. Your OAuth tokens, GitHub User ID/username, display name, avatar URL, and account session details are stored locally on your device in private app storage to support multi-account switching. FastGit accesses repository information, pull requests, issues, releases, and workflows solely to display and manage them at your request. Vineyard Tech does not store, copy, or transfer your GitHub tokens or private source code to any Vineyard Tech servers.
  • Keystore Files & Build Secrets: If you utilize the automated Keystore secret propagation feature, your saved .jks keystore profiles, aliases, and passwords are stored strictly locally in your device's private database. When propagating secrets to a target repository, the credentials are encrypted directly on your device using standard Libsodium / NaCl Sealed Box encryption and uploaded directly to GitHub's Actions Secrets API endpoints. We have no access to your private signing keys or build passwords.
  • Contact & Support Inquiries: When you reach out to us via the in-app "Contact Us" screen, the information you submit (Name, Email address, and Message) is transmitted and processed through our form service provider, Formspree (formspree.io), strictly to receive and respond to your support requests, inquiries, or bug reports.
  • Local Logs & Crash Diagnostics: FastGit includes a local diagnostic console ("Live Logs") that tracks session activity and crash errors inside your device's isolated app storage. These logs stay completely local to your device unless you explicitly use the "Save File" button to export them to your Downloads folder.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

FastGit displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. To provide ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain device and diagnostic information, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID
  • IP address and approximate location (general country/city level)
  • App interactions (such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions)
  • Performance diagnostics (such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage)

This data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. For more details on how Google uses this data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Account & Data Deletion (Retention & User Controls)

Vineyard Tech provides both instant on-device controls and web/email-based deletion options for FastGit users, without requiring you to reinstall the app.

How to Request Account Access Revocation & Data Deletion (Without Reinstalling FastGit)

  1. Revoke FastGit OAuth Access on GitHub (Web): Visit https://github.com/settings/applications (Authorized OAuth Apps), select FastGit, and click Revoke. This immediately terminates all FastGit access to your GitHub account.
  2. Request Deletion of Support & Associated Records (Email): Send an email to contact@vineyardtech.online or josymvine@gmail.com with the subject line FastGit Account and Data Deletion Request and include the email address or GitHub username associated with your inquiry.

Processing Timeframe: All verified deletion requests for support/contact records are completed within 30 days of receipt, with email confirmation sent once deleted.

In-App Partial & Local Data Deletion Controls

  • Clear Cached Data: Open FastGit and go to Settings → App → Clear Cache to delete cached repository trees and temporary local data without signing out.
  • Sign Out / Remove Accounts: Go to Settings → Auth → Log Out (or use the Account Switcher Edit mode) to remove one or all stored GitHub OAuth tokens and session records from your device immediately.
  • Delete Saved Keystore Profiles: Go to Settings → Keys to delete any locally stored Keystore profiles.
  • Uninstall App: Uninstalling FastGit permanently deletes all local tokens, Room databases, Keystore profiles, and cached files stored in app private storage.

Data Deleted, Data Retained & Retention Periods

  • Data Deleted Immediately: Local GitHub OAuth tokens, User ID/username session data, Room cache entries, saved Keystore profiles, and internal Live Logs are permanently erased immediately upon Log Out, Clear Cache, or app uninstallation.
  • Support Inquiries (Formspree): Name, email address, and message content submitted via the Contact Us screen are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry (up to 90 days if inactive), or deleted within 30 days upon your deletion request.
  • Data Not Controlled by Vineyard Tech: Your GitHub account, repositories, commits, and files reside on GitHub's servers and remain unaffected unless you delete them directly on github.com. Advertising and diagnostic telemetry processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's retention policies; you may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID anytime via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Security of Your Information

All external network communication performed by FastGit—including requests to the GitHub REST API, Google AdMob services, and Formspree support endpoints—is conducted strictly over secure, encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal information or user code to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

FastGit is a professional developer utility intended for software developers and individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
